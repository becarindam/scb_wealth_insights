Top driver: Campaigns / 30 days

Customers receiving 7 or more campaigns in a trailing 30-day window show a CTR of 1.4%, against 6.5% for customers receiving 1–2. This driver alone accounts for roughly twice the impact of the next-ranked factor. This is a correlation-adjusted ranking, not proof of causation — see Recommendations for how the pilot validates it before scale.
